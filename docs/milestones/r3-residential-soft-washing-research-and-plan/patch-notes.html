Residential soft-washing research: evidence round

November 2, 2026Ready for review

Overview

We finished the evidence-gathering stage for the residential soft-washing campaign in Essex County, with Nutley, Livingston and Verona as priority towns. It combines a year of Google Search data for three more website pages, Google's keyword estimates for each area, and a fresh look at which competitors show up on Google Maps and in search results. Nothing on the website changed. These findings feed the next step: a soft-washing plan and a short list of questions for Kyle.

Added

  • A competitor snapshot for house washing, roof cleaning and soft washing, with map searches centered on Nutley, Livingston, Verona and the middle of Essex County. It shows who appears on the map, in the top three local listings and in the regular results, and where Jones appears today.
  • Google keyword estimates for Essex County, Nutley, Livingston and Verona, kept separately for each area, plus a sorted list of the extra keyword ideas Google suggested.
  • A list of six open questions for the next stage. Four need Kyle's answer: roof cleaning, gutter cleaning, reviews and the Business Profile, and pricing guidance.

Improved

  • The soft-washing keyword research now covers the homepage, the Additional Services page and the Essex County page, not just the soft-washing, Livingston and Montclair pages.
  • The page plan is clearer. The homepage competes with the soft-washing page for Essex house-washing searches, the Additional Services page mostly attracts gutter-cleaning searches, and the Essex County page received no Google impressions in the past year.

What happens next

  • Kyle's answers on roof cleaning, gutter cleaning, reviews and pricing will shape the soft-washing plan.
  • The next stage turns this research into a prioritized plan for the soft-washing pages before the spring season.
