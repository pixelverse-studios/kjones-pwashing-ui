Residential soft-washing research and next-phase plan

November 2, 2026Ready for review

Overview

The research for the residential soft-washing campaign in Essex County is finished, with Nutley, Livingston and Verona as priority towns. It has been turned into a proposed plan and a short meeting agenda for Kyle. The research combines a year of Google Search data for the key website pages, Google's keyword estimates for each area, and a fresh look at which competitors show up on Google Maps and in search results. Nothing on the website has changed yet: the plan and its first page brief need Kyle's answers and approval first.

Added

  • A proposed soft-washing plan. It covers who the campaign is for, which existing page should handle each kind of customer search, which searches the campaign deliberately skips, and the order of work from now through spring 2027.
  • A step-by-step way to decide how roof cleaning is presented, so no web address has to change before Kyle decides how he sells it.
  • A meeting agenda for Kyle: facts to confirm, decisions that change the plan, the specific photos, projects and reviews each page needs, and who approves each step.
  • A competitor snapshot for house washing, roof cleaning and soft washing, with map searches centered on Nutley, Livingston, Verona and the middle of Essex County.
  • Google keyword estimates for Essex County, Nutley, Livingston and Verona, kept separately for each area, plus a sorted list of the extra keyword ideas Google suggested.

Improved

  • The keyword research now covers the homepage, the Additional Services page and the Essex County page, as well as the soft-washing, Livingston and Montclair pages.
  • The page plan reflects what the new data shows. The soft-washing page is Jones's main foothold in search. The homepage competes with it for Essex house-washing searches. The Additional Services page is mostly found for gutter cleaning. The Essex County page received no Google impressions in the past year.
  • The September schedule has been replaced with a current one that keeps the holiday-lighting fix first.

What happens next

  • Review the plan and agenda with Kyle and collect his answers on services, roof cleaning, audience, towns, gutter cleaning, reviews and pricing.
  • Kyle gathers before-and-after photos, project details and reviews for the soft-washing and Livingston pages.
  • Once Kyle's facts are approved, a brief for the improved soft-washing page is written for approval before any website change.
