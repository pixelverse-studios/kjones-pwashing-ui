01 / SUMMARY

The release in three decisions

One defined service

Seasonal Christmas and holiday light installation for homeowners. The confirmed high-level service includes Jones-supplied installation materials, maintenance, removal, and storage.

One clear territory

Essex County, plus Paramus and Bergen County communities south of Paramus. The Bergen page no longer suggests countywide coverage.

One suitable lead path

Holiday requests go to a dedicated Lavo form. The existing instant-quote tool serves pressure washing and was not suitable for this service.

02 / PROCESS

How we arrived at the SEO and wording

  1. Start with the existing pages and search evidence

    We audited the holiday hub and both county pages, then reviewed page-filtered Google Search Console exports. In the supplied prior-year window, Essex had 609 impressions and Bergen had 106, both with zero clicks. Much of the visible query mix was for broad outdoor, permanent, electrical, or security lighting, so clearer service boundaries mattered more than adding more copy.

  2. Check how customers describe the job

    We compared Search Console queries with Keyword Planner, Google Trends, and sampled search results and competitors. “Christmas light installation” was the clearest primary phrase for the Essex service page. “Holiday lighting” remains a natural supporting term across the site. We kept the existing county URLs and did not create thin town pages or target every phrase containing “lighting.”

  3. Confirm business facts with Kyle

    The September 27 meeting and ADM-42 established the homeowner-only seasonal offer, supplied materials, included work, service boundary, and quote-first pricing approach. We treated products, exact ownership terms, repair timing, guarantees, calendar capacity, and specific project claims as unknown unless supported separately.

  4. Write from a claim-and-proof brief

    The Essex content brief mapped each page section to a customer decision and a supported fact. We removed commercial and permanent-lighting positioning, unsupported Montclair and Maplewood stories, broad town-by-town filler, an unverified public price and price schema, and urgency or operational promises we could not substantiate.

  5. Validate the full request and search path

    We connected the dedicated Lavo form, tested a labeled submission and business receipt, checked mobile sizing and confirmation behavior, then verified the live pages’ status, index settings, canonicals, sitemap entries, and Service/Breadcrumb structured data. Search Console and CRM outcomes will be monitored separately; a page view or CTA click is not a completed lead.

04 / FOR DESIGN

What matters for Sami’s next pass

Design around the homeowner’s decision

  • Make the service, location boundary, inclusions, and request action obvious above the fold.
  • Let the Essex and Bergen pages feel related while giving each a distinct geographic role.
  • Account for the light-colored third-party Lavo form inside the dark site; its internal fields and consent styling are controlled in Lavo.
  • Keep the call option, keyboard focus, mobile layout, and form fallback easy to find.

Keep proof and claims honest

We removed unverified project stories. Please do not present stock or generated lighting imagery as completed Jones work. Permissioned project photos, reviews, and job details can be added when Kyle provides them. The dedicated seasonal homepage promotion is also still open design work.
