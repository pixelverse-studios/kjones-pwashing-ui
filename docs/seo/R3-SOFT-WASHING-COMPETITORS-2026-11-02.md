## 6. Implications for the next-phase plan (inputs to SEO-39 and SEO-40)

These are inputs for the plan, not decisions.

1. **Jones is effectively absent from map and local-pack results for its own services, even in Nutley.** On a map, the site can't fix that by itself. The Business Profile matters more: review volume, services listed, photos and service area. Kyle needs to decide how much effort goes into getting reviews and keeping the Business Profile active as part of this campaign.
2. **The county-level core page is Jones's only organic foothold.** `/services/soft-washing` reached page 1 for `soft washing essex county nj`, which matches the GSC evidence (GSC-SW-02/03). That supports improving the core page first rather than creating new URLs.
3. **The Livingston page doesn't reach page 1 for its own target** (`soft washing livingston nj`). Specialist competitors have dedicated, project-backed town pages. Improve it with real local proof before adding Nutley or Verona pages.
4. **"House washing" is ambiguous on Google.** For Livingston, the results are almost entirely interior cleaning. Titles, H1s and descriptions need to say **exterior** or **siding** explicitly.
5. **Roof cleaning is a distinct, contested task.** Roofers compete for it, and specialists win with dedicated roof pages (Softwash Ranger, Under Pressure, TruClean). Kyle's roof-cleaning offer and which page owns it are now the main open question for that search group.
6. **Competitors to track in later checks:** North Jersey Property Service (Nutley), Rinse Prince, NJ Wash Co., Softwash Ranger, Gallo Bros, WeatherX, TruClean, Aqua Pro, S&D Power Washing.

## 7. Limitations

- One day, desktop only, signed out. Mobile results and different times of day weren't sampled.
- Pinning the map center isn't the same as having a device physically in the town. The organic-search location came from the browser's IP address.
- The `soft washing` map search at the Essex County center wasn't collected.
- The Maps limited view shows about the top 10 businesses, so a business outside that set may still rank lower down.
- Competitor service areas, categories and claims were recorded as displayed and not independently verified.
