**Status:** Active — research and planning

## Objective

Build an evidence-backed SEO plan that increases qualified residential quote opportunities for Jones Pressure Washing without publishing generic location content, unsupported claims, or changes that cannot be measured.

The immediate objective is to determine what the existing holiday-lighting service hub and county pages should own, what customers need from them, and what must be corrected or strengthened before the 2026 holiday season.

## Working guardrails

- Research and audits remain read-only until implementation is explicitly approved.
- Do not create Linear implementation tickets unless requested.
- Do not commit or push without explicit approval.
- Preserve existing URLs until query, conversion, backlink, and replacement evidence supports a change.
- Keep organic, Google Maps/local-pack, directory, marketplace, and aggregator competitors separate.
- Treat missing owner-controlled or private data as unknown, not zero.
- Do not copy competitor wording, FAQs, pages, pricing, guarantees, schema, location lists, or backlink tactics.
- Prefer improving the correct existing page before creating another URL.

### Material findings so far

- Organic-result competitors and Google Maps competitors are materially different groups.
- The strongest local competitor content is grounded in identifiable projects, real operating details, and clear service boundaries.
- Several sampled location pages were generic, repetitive, or used media from another market.
- Holiday-lighting customers appear to evaluate the complete seasonal relationship: design, installation, maintenance, repair, takedown, storage, and the following season.
- Oxidation removal appears to be a distinct diagnostic and specialty-service need, but local demand and JPW capability remain unverified.
- Bergen County has genuine solar-panel-cleaning competitors, but performance, frequency, warranty, and safety claims require stronger evidence.
- Public Trends evidence suggests `Christmas light installation` may be more common than `holiday light installation`, but the state-level sample is too sparse to make a page decision by itself.
