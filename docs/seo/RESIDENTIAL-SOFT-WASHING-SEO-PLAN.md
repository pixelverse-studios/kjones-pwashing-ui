# Jones Pressure Washing Residential Soft-Washing SEO Plan

**Status:** Proposed for review; no implementation approved  
**Prepared:** 2026-09-17  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-25  
**Campaign:** Residential soft washing  
**Primary market:** Essex County, New Jersey  
**Priority towns:** Nutley, Livingston, and Verona

## 1. Objective and success definition

Increase qualified residential soft-washing inquiries from Essex County, with special attention to Nutley, Livingston, and Verona.

The campaign should help homeowners, residential property managers, and residential HOAs understand the correct service, trust Jones Pressure Washing, and complete a quote request or connected phone call.

Success is not a ranking report alone. Evaluate the campaign through:

- relevant non-branded Search Console visibility and clicks
- organic entrances to the correct service and location pages
- confirmed quote-form submissions and connected calls
- qualified leads requesting an eligible residential service in the target geography
- estimates, booked work, and collected revenue when reliable attribution exists

## 2. Approved scope

### Included

- residential soft washing
- house and siding washing performed with an appropriate soft-wash method
- roof cleaning or roof washing performed with an appropriate low-pressure method
- Essex County, especially Nutley, Livingston, and Verona
- homeowners, residential property managers, and residential HOAs
- quote-form and phone-call conversion paths
- year-round demand, with seasonality measured rather than assumed

### Excluded from this campaign

- pressure-washing promotion
- holiday-lighting promotion
- commercial soft-washing acquisition
- Bergen County keyword expansion
- oxidation removal or solar-panel cleaning until separately approved and operationally verified
- generic city-page expansion outside the three priority towns

Campaign scope does not by itself prove that the business has stopped serving excluded services or markets. Do not remove accurate sitewide information solely because it is outside this campaign.

## 9. Measurement contract

Use these stages consistently:

1. **Intent signal:** CTA click, phone-link click, or form start.
2. **Lead:** confirmed form receipt or connected call.
3. **Qualified lead:** eligible residential soft-washing need at a serviceable Essex County property with usable contact and property information.
4. **Estimate or opportunity:** Jones reviews and prices the work.
5. **Booked work:** customer accepts and schedules.
6. **Completed work:** service is delivered.
7. **Revenue:** label quoted, booked, invoiced, or collected rather than treating them as interchangeable.

Initial visibility measures can be reviewed after Google recrawls the affected page and sufficient data accumulates. Qualified leads, bookings, and revenue require a window appropriate to normal sales and job-completion latency. Observation windows are checkpoints, not promises that rankings or leads will appear by a fixed date.

### Proposed reporting stack

- **Google Search Console:** search impressions, clicks, CTR, page ownership, query themes, country, and device evidence.
- **GA4:** organic landing sessions, engagement, observable site actions, and validated key events.
- **Lavo CRM or owner-supplied aggregates:** confirmed inquiries, quotes, qualification, booked and completed jobs, invoices, and collected revenue.
- **Google Business Profile:** profile visibility and interactions kept distinct from ordinary website organic traffic.
- **Google Data Studio (formerly Looker Studio):** client-facing dashboard combining the supported GA4 and Search Console views, plus a privacy-safe Google Sheet or supported connector for approved Lavo or business-outcome aggregates.

GA4 and Search Console are sufficient for the initial website and organic-search measurement layer. They are not sufficient by themselves for confirmed calls, form receipts inside the cross-domain Lavo iframe, lead quality, booked jobs, or revenue. Do not purchase another general website analytics product by default. Add a call, CRM, local-rank, or data-warehouse tool only when it closes a defined decision-relevant gap.

Use custom GA4 events for observable interactions such as quote-modal opens, phone clicks, and email clicks. Use `generate_lead` only when a confirmed form receipt or equivalent lead creation is verifiably observed. Do not send personal information to GA4 event names, parameters, URLs, or custom dimensions.
