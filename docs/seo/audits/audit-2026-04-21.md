## Summary

The site has a solid technical foundation and is materially stronger than a typical small local-service site. Core metadata, canonical tags, JSON-LD coverage, county hubs, city landers, and service-to-location linking are all present. The main problems are now operational and local-search related rather than foundational build issues.

The two highest-leverage issues are:
- fix the remaining schema/documentation drift in the codebase
- improve GBP quality and activity so local visibility is supported by a stronger off-site/local signal set

GSC indicates early traction, but not strong ranking yet: `15 clicks`, `1.02k impressions`, `1.5% CTR`, `22.3 average position`, with `13 indexed` and `14 not indexed`. GBP is verified and has `6 Google reviews` at `5.0`, but engagement is weak: roughly `108 business views`, fewer than `50` search appearances, `7` profile interactions, `1` call, and `6` website clicks from March 2026 through April 21, 2026.

- Indexed pages: `13`
- Not indexed: `14`
- Reasons shown:
  - `10` discovered - currently not indexed
  - `2` crawled - currently not indexed

Interpretation:
- “Discovered - currently not indexed” is the main risk. Google knows about a material chunk of the site but is not prioritizing crawl/index for those URLs yet.

- This pattern usually points to a combination of freshness, authority, crawl prioritization, or insufficient demand/signals rather than broken technical setup alone.
