## 3. What the evidence says (November 2)

1. **Jones is nearly invisible locally.** In a one-day, signed-out desktop snapshot, it does not appear in any of 11 Google Maps searches centered on Nutley, Livingston, Verona, and central Essex, or in any of 9 local "top three" listings. Its Business Profile has 6 reviews; competitors that keep appearing have 12 to 441. *(Competitor research §2–3)*
2. **The soft-washing page is the only real foothold.** It shows on page one for "soft washing essex county nj" and sits around position 9.5–9.9 for Essex vinyl and stucco siding searches, without clicks yet. *(Competitor research §2; keyword research GSC-SW-02/03)*
3. **The homepage picks up Essex house-washing searches** that belong to the soft-washing page, mostly worded as "power washing." *(GSC-HOME-02)*
4. **The Essex County page had no Google impressions in 12 months**, even though it's set up to be found. The cause still needs to be checked. *(GSC-ESX-01)*
5. **The Additional Services page is mostly found for gutter cleaning.** Its roof-cleaning visibility is small and mostly outside the service area. *(GSC-ADD-01/02)*
6. **Roof cleaning is a real, separate customer task.** It shows up in every area's keyword data, has the highest ad bids in the Essex data, and competitors that do well have dedicated roof pages. Roofers also compete for those searches. *(KWP-03; competitor research §5–6)*
7. **"House washing" is ambiguous on Google.** In Livingston it mostly returns interior house cleaners, so pages must say *exterior* plainly. *(Competitor research §5)*
8. **There is no reported town-level keyword data** for Nutley, Livingston, or Verona terms. Keyword Planner gives only rough ranges for this account, so it can't justify or rule out town pages. *(KWP-01/02)*
9. **Customers ask about cost** often enough that pricing guidance is worth covering. *(KWP-06)*

## 7. How results will be measured, and the limits

- **Google Search Console** shows which searches each page appears for and the clicks it gets.
- **GA4** (added to the site in October with cookie consent; live data collection still to be confirmed) shows site visits and actions such as opening the quote form. It cannot see whether the quote form inside the Lavo window was actually submitted.
- **Lavo** is where real outcomes live: quote requests, quotes sent, booked and completed jobs, and revenue. A monthly summary from Kyle (no customer details) is the most reliable way to connect the website to booked work.
- Keyword Planner numbers are rough ranges and ad-market signals, not promises of traffic.
- Rankings, traffic, leads, and revenue are tracked outcomes. None of them is guaranteed by any date in this plan.
